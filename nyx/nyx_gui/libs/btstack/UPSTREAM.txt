Selected sources and headers; Nova-specific modifications are documented below. Personal experimental build.

Nova23 modification: classic/hid_host.c optionally opens known HID PSM17/19 without SDP (NOVA_HID_KNOWN_PSMS), skips incoming descriptor query, and cleans up immediate channel failures and a failed interrupt channel while control is open. Generic behavior is unchanged when the macro is absent. License retained.

Nova26 modification (NOVA_HID_KNOWN_PSMS only): when paired report-mode HID opens cross, a peer-initiated control/interrupt channel supersedes an outgoing channel still awaiting completion. Existing HID CID retained; established/duplicate incoming channels are not replaced. CHANNEL_OPENED callbacks match canonical L2CAP CID plus peer, so late results of superseded channels cannot finalize the active session. Late successful noncanonical channels are disconnected; other superseded entries follow L2CAP response/RTX cleanup without modifying L2CAP code. Full vendor diff supplied.
