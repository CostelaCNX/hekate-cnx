Nova modifications, all under NOVA_HID_KNOWN_PSMS in classic/hid_host.c; generic
behavior is unchanged when the macro is absent:
- Known HID PSM 17/19 are opened without an SDP query; the incoming descriptor
  query is skipped; immediate channel failures and a failed interrupt channel
  while control is open are cleaned up.
- When paired report-mode HID opens cross, a peer-initiated control/interrupt
  channel supersedes an outgoing channel still awaiting completion. CHANNEL_OPENED
  callbacks match the canonical L2CAP CID plus peer, so late results of superseded
  channels cannot finalize the active session; late successful noncanonical
  channels are disconnected.
